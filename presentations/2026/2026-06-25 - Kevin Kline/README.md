# Futureproof Your Data & Analytics Career with AI

* **Speaker:** Kevin Kline
* **Date:** 2026-06-25
* **Meeting:** Music City Data User Group — [event on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/music-city-data-user-group/events/315317990)

## Abstract

What are the technical skills that are necessary for a database professional to
succeed in today's workforce? Is SQL still king? More importantly, what technical
skills are needed as our organizations undergo a massive transformation in the face
of GenAI and cloud migrations? Let's discuss skills and capabilities that will make
your Data & Analytics (D&A) career fireproof and futureproof.

In this session, we will discuss the technical skills needed to succeed as a DBA, a
Data Engineer, and an Analytics professional. In addition to tech skills, we will
spend time discussing the interpersonal skills held in common by successful IT
professionals enabling them to avoid downsizing and better compete for the very best
jobs against other talented peers.

This combination of technical skills for today and the future, plus soft skills that
are perennially sought after will help you become irreplaceable in your workplace and
can lead to a fireproof and futureproof career. Learn them today!
